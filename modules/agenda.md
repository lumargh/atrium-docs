# Agenda

With the card picked out, v opens the view menu, and a view's first letter then picks it: v l the list, v t the timeline, v w the week, v m the month. On the timeline, - and = zoom out and in, instead of resizing its height, and c scrolls to the current time. Tracks and episodes played (lines tagged `#song` or `#podcast`, as Music and Podcasts log them) sit as small icons in a lane at the timeline's right, and fold to a count at the end of the list; pointing at an icon names them, a click plays them again, and right-click → **Add to timeline** makes one an item as long as it lasted. Enter goes to the first item in sight, and the arrows from item to item; on an item, Enter opens it, x marks it done, r reschedules it (t today, m tomorrow, w next week, d a date), Alt and the arrows move it by a day or 15 minutes (with Shift, its end), p times it, e renames it, Delete deletes it and m shows its menu, each also a command.
