# Atrium Complete

Atrium is free and open source. **Atrium Complete** adds the Terminal, Recorder, Music, Web page, Podcasts and Habits cards and video backgrounds. They work free for 30 days; after that, they need a license key: $14.99, once, from [Polar](https://buy.polar.sh/polar_cl_y0kZRpPkmgPvNxV0jekPBHkZdU4a5oBNyric248vSAY). The first 1,000 keys are free.

To use a key, choose **Enter license key** in Atrium's settings, on a locked card or as a command. The key is checked once online, then Atrium works offline. Without a key, every other card keeps working as before.
