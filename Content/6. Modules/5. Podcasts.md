# Podcasts

Laid out as [Music](music) is, the episode playing over its show's art, with tabs for **Episodes**, **Transcript** and **Shows**. Episodes lists the newest of the shows in your subscriptions note (`Podcasts.md` unless set in the card's settings), newest first. Shows shows their covers, a click opening a show's episodes and its button playing the newest; typing searches [Podverse](https://podverse.fm), and a result's button subscribes, adding its feed link to the note. A click plays an episode from where you left off, with jumps back 15 and forward 30 seconds, a playback speed, **Mark as played**, **Download** and **Show notes**. A downloaded episode is saved in a folder named after the subscriptions note, beside it (`Podcasts/<Show>/`), and plays from there. Transcript shows the episode's transcript where its feed has one (Podcasting 2.0), the line being spoken lit and followed; a click goes to that line. How far you got is a line in today's daily note, such as `- [8:12 AM] Listened to [Show — Episode](<address>) 23:41 / 1:02:10 #podcast`, updated as you listen and ending in ✓ once played; the last 90 days of daily notes are read back to resume. Starting an episode pauses Music, and Music starting pauses the episode. On the card, p plays or pauses, and - and = jump back 15 and forward 30 seconds.
