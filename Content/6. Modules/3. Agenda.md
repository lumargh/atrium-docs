# Agenda

With the card picked out, v opens the view menu, and a view's first letter then picks it: v l the list, v t the timeline, v w the week, v m the month. On the timeline, - and = zoom out and in, instead of resizing its height, and c scrolls to the current time. Tracks and episodes played (lines tagged `#song` or `#podcast`, as Music and Podcasts log them) sit as small icons in a lane at the timeline's right, and fold to a count at the end of the list; pointing at an icon names them, a click plays them again, and right-click → **Add to timeline** makes one an item as long as it lasted. Enter goes to the first item in sight, and the arrows from item to item; on an item, Enter opens it, x marks it done, r reschedules it (t today, m tomorrow, w next week, d a date), Alt and the arrows move it by a day or 15 minutes (with Shift, its end), p times it, e renames it, n opens a calendar event's note, Delete deletes it and m shows its menu, each also a command. A macOS calendar event the day already has a line for, at the same time and starting with its title, shows once, as that line. A macOS calendar event gets a note of its own from the note button beside it, its right-click menu, n or the command: `2026-10-22 11.00 Standup` for a single event, or one `Standup -R` for a repeating one, kept named and timed as the event changes in Calendar. **Create notes for new events**, in the card's settings, makes them for events starting from when it's turned on.
