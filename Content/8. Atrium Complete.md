# Atrium Complete

Atrium is free and open source. **Atrium Complete** adds the Terminal, Recorder, Music, Web page, Podcasts and Habits cards and video backgrounds. They start locked: **Start 30-day trial** unlocks them for 30 days, and after that they need a license key: $14.99, once, from [Polar](https://buy.polar.sh/polar_cl_y0kZRpPkmgPvNxV0jekPBHkZdU4a5oBNyric248vSAY?discount_code=EARLYBIRD). The first 1,000 keys are free with the code `EARLYBIRD`, filled in by that link.

Both **Start 30-day trial** and **Enter license key** are in Atrium's settings, on a locked card, on its right-click menu and as commands. The key is checked once online, then Atrium works offline. Without a key, every other card keeps working as before.
