# Stacks

Several cards can share one place and show one at a time. Pick out the cards (click one's frame, then Cmd/Ctrl- or Shift-click the others) and choose **Stack these** from their right-click menu or the command, or press :. The card showing has a faint +1 (or more) after its name; point at its header and the other cards' names slide out: before its name, one for each other kind of card in the stack (Tasks, Calendar), and after it, the other cards of its own kind, such as other notes. A click on one brings it forward. } and { bring the next or previous one forward from the keyboard, and Option+} and Option+{ move the card showing later or earlier in the stack's order. **Take out of stack**, or Option+; (…), puts the card showing beside the stack, the next one taking its place; removing it does the same. Any cards can share a stack.
