# Network use

If you set a weather location, Atrium sends it to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) to look up a city's coordinates and fetch the forecast. If you enter coordinates instead, they are sent to OpenStreetMap's [Nominatim](https://nominatim.org) to find the place name. A Web page card loads the page set in its settings, and whatever that page loads, directly from the site. A Podcasts card fetches the feeds in its subscriptions note and plays episodes from where they're hosted, and searching for shows sends what you type to [Podverse](https://podverse.fm). Nothing else leaves your vault, and weather is off until a location is set. Dictation in a Recorder card is transcribed on your Mac and never sent anywhere.
